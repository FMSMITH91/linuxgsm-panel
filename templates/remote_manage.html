      The busiest attacking IPs on this host by failed attempts over the last 7 days (current + rotated logs). Block firewalls an IP on all ports (UFW) — stronger than a fail2ban ban, which only covers the attacked service. Auto-block firewalls every IP at or above the threshold and releases each once its 7-day count drops back below — a cumulative count a slow attacker who spaces attempts to dodge fail2ban's short window can't escape.

(Game-server ports have no fail2ban jail, so this never covers those.)
