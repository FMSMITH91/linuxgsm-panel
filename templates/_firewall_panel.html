What these settings do

Incoming and outgoing are what UFW does with a connection no rule matches; routed is traffic this host forwards to other machines. Deny drops it without a reply, reject refuses it. The panel refuses any change that would lock SSH or the panel itself out of this host, and turning the firewall off makes the host accept every connection.

Rate limiting refuses an address that opens more than 6 connections in 30 seconds. Right for anything someone logs into (SSH, RCON); wrong for gameplay traffic, where that is a quiet evening.
